# Hi, I’m Raya Ru

I’m a Product and Interaction Designer studying Computation and Design at Duke Kunshan University. I work across AI product thinking, user research, interaction prototyping, and creative technology.

My current interests include human–AI collaboration, generative identity systems, music experiences, and computational approaches to cultural expression.

## Selected projects

- [PROFILEd](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/profiled) — a three-system study of how human and AI control shapes generative digital impressions.
- [AI Playlist Copilot](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/ai-playlist-copilot) — a product concept for finding music in a personal Spotify library through natural-language intent.
- [AIgoRhythm](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/aigorhythm) — an AI-assisted music improvisation tool focused on interaction flow and users’ sense of presence.
- [The City as System](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/city-as-system) — six computational art studies using generative rules, data, image transformation, and sound to explore urban memory.
