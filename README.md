## Selected work

### [PROFILEd](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/profiled) · Generative identity and human–AI control

**Question:** Who authors a digital impression when a person, a set of rules, and AI all shape its representation?

- Designed a branching Qualtrics research flow around context, disclosure, and control, then translated responses into visual rules and interactive views.
- Built and compared three control models: human-defined mappings, human–AI collaboration, and algorithm-led selection. Later JavaScript prototypes explore individual views, evidence-based comparison, and revisable representations.
- Designed comparison links to show only explicit shared values, choices, or reasons; the system does not infer compatibility from visual proximity.

**My contribution:** Research framing, survey design, interaction logic, visual mappings, and prototyping. **Status:** Ongoing research prototype; participant responses and unapproved materials remain private. [Read the case study →](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/profiled)

### [AI Playlist Copilot](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/ai-playlist-copilot) · Natural-language music retrieval

**Problem:** A listener may know the mood, activity, and time available, but not the song title or artist to search for.

- Defined an MVP that turns a plain-language request into a playlist drawn from the listener's own Spotify library.
- Created the PRD, information architecture, user/task flows, UX copy, wireframes, and a responsive Next.js/TypeScript interface covering onboarding, library sync, prompt entry, results, history, and recovery states.
- Examined failure cases such as ambiguous intent and plausible results that miss the user's actual goal; proposed clarification and result explanations for the product flow.

**My contribution:** 0→1 product definition, interaction design, and front-end prototype. **Status:** The current interface uses mock data; Spotify authorization, library sync, and AI parsing are planned integrations. [Read the case study →](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/ai-playlist-copilot)

### [The City as System](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/city-as-system) · Six computational art studies

**Theme:** What can urban systems reveal about encounters, history, perception, and memory?

- Built six Processing studies using generative motion, image transformation, particle systems, music data, and sound interaction.
- Turned one city photograph into an interactive image of approximately 2,500 particles, with stress, energy, order, and warmth controlled by cursor position.
- Mapped 20 songs to a skyline and built a Shanghai Metro simulation in which train movement, passenger behavior, sound, and personal imagery unfold together.

**My contribution:** Concept, rules, data-to-visual mappings, code, and audiovisual interaction. **Status:** Completed course portfolio; the repository documents selected work. [Explore the six studies →](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/city-as-system)

User feedback informed the interaction flow and positioning; the project reached the final round of DKU's Dii Innovation Incubator. [Read more →](https://github.com/rayaruyi0320-svg/aigorhythm)
